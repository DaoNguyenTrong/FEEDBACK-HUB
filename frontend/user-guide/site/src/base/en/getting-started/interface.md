# Find your way around

This page introduces the fixed parts of the app and how to make it comfortable to read: light or dark theme, font size and language. These choices are remembered on the device you use.

## The sidebar

The left sidebar shows the app logo (click it to go to **Home**) and the main sections, in groups:

| Group            | Entries                                                             |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (top of the bar) | **Home**                                                            |
| **Organization** | **Members**, **Organization management**, **Audit log**             |
| **System**       | **Organizations** (only a FeedbackHub system administrator sees it) |

An entry you don't have permission to open is not shown. The entries of the **Organization** group only appear while you are working in an organization.

The sidebar follows the screen size:

- Wide screens: icons and labels.
- Medium screens: narrow, icons only. Point at an icon to see the entry's name.
- Phones: the sidebar is hidden. Click **Open menu** at the top left to slide the menu in from the left. It has a **Go to…** box and the same entries, and it closes when you choose one.

On a phone, **Open menu** sits at the start of the top bar, and the **Go to…** box shrinks to a magnifier icon.

## The organization switcher

The button at the top left shows the organization you work in, or **Personal workspace** when no organization is selected. Click it to open the **Your organizations** list, pick another organization, go back to the welcome page or create a new organization. The details are in the **Organizations** chapter.

## The account menu

Click your avatar at the top right. The menu has:

- your name and email;
- **Profile**;
- **Appearance**: opens the **Appearance settings** panel; the color mode in use is shown on the right;
- **Language**: choose **Tiếng Việt** or **English**;
- **User guide**: opens this guide in a new tab, in the language you use;
- **Logout**.

## Change the font size

1. Click your avatar, then choose **Appearance**. The **Appearance settings** panel opens on the right.
2. Under **Font size**, choose **Increase font size** or **Decrease font size**. Click **Reset font size** to go back to the default.

Color mode and language are in the same panel.

## Change the language

Click your avatar, then choose **Tiếng Việt** or **English** under **Language**. All text in the app switches to the other language at once, without reloading the page.

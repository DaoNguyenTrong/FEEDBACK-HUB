# Adjust the appearance

You can make the app easier to read and use: light or dark mode, the language and the font size. These choices are remembered on the device you use.

## Steps

1. Click your avatar at the top right, then choose **Appearance**. The **Appearance settings** panel opens on the right.
2. Choose one or more of the options below. Changes apply at once, with nothing to save.
3. Click outside the panel or press **Esc** to close it.

| Option         | Choices                                                                               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Color mode** | **Light**, **Dark** or **System**                                                     |
| **Language**   | **Tiếng Việt** or **English**                                                         |
| **Font size**  | **Decrease font size**, **Increase font size** or **Reset font size** for the default |

You can also change the language in the account menu, under **Language**.

## Jump to a page

Press **Ctrl K** (or **⌘ K** on a Mac), or click the **Go to…** box on the top bar. Type the name of a page or an action, then press **Enter**. Besides pages, the box offers actions such as **Appearance settings**, **Profile**, **Switch language** and **Logout**.

## Result

The appearance changes at once and stays that way the next time you open the app in the same browser.

# Organization management

The **Organization management** page lets you rename the organization you work in, or deactivate it when it is no longer used.

## Before you start

- You have selected the right organization (see [switch organization](./switch)).

## Rename the organization

1. Click **Organization management** in the sidebar (the **Organization** group).
2. Under **Organization details**, edit the **Organization name** box.
3. Click **Save**.

The **Short identifier** is shown below it and can't be changed.

## Deactivate the organization

Deactivating suspends all activity in the organization. Data is kept. Only a FeedbackHub system administrator can reactivate it.

1. Under **Deactivate organization**, click the **Deactivate organization** button. The **Deactivate …?** dialog opens.
2. Read "The whole organization is suspended right away:":
   - The widget of every product stops receiving feedback, and customers' private links are suspended.
   - No one can enter the organization, including you.
   - Data is kept. Only a FeedbackHub system administrator can reactivate it.
3. Enter a **Reason** (optional, up to 500 characters).
4. Click **Deactivate organization**. Click **Cancel** to discard.

If you are also a FeedbackHub system administrator, the **Reason** box is required, and you can reactivate the organization in the **Organizations** list.

## Result

- Renaming: the app says "Renamed to …", and the new name shows in the organization switcher.
- Deactivating: the app takes you to the [welcome page](./welcome). The organization appears dimmed with a **Suspended** tag; its members can no longer enter it.

## Common problems

- **"Enter the organization name."**: the **Organization name** box is empty.
- **"Enter a reason."**: you are a FeedbackHub system administrator, so the **Reason** is required.
- **You don't see Organization management in the sidebar**: your account doesn't have the **Manage organization** permission in this organization.

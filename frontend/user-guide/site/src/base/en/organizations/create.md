# Create an organization

Create an organization when your team or unit needs a shared workspace. Whoever creates it becomes its **Owner**.

## Before you start

- Your email is [verified](../getting-started/verify-email).

## Steps

1. Click the button with the organization's name at the top left, then click **Create organization**. On the [welcome page](./welcome), click **Create a new organization**.
2. Enter the **Organization name**.
3. Check the **Short identifier**. The app suggests one from the name you just typed. You can edit it; once you type in this box yourself, the name no longer overwrites it.
4. Click **Create organization**.

The **Short identifier** uses only a–z, 0–9 and "-", starts and ends with a letter or digit, has no two "-" in a row, and is 3 to 50 characters long. It can't be changed after creation.

## Result

The page reloads and you land in the new organization; its name shows at the top left. The organization comes with three roles: **Owner**, **Admin** and **Member**, and you hold the **Owner** role.

## Common problems

- **"This identifier is already taken."**: another organization uses this identifier. Choose a different one.
- **"Verify your email before doing this."**: click **Resend verification email**, open the link in the email, then try again.
- **"Enter the organization name."**: the **Organization name** box is empty.

# The welcome page

The welcome page is where you choose the organization to work in. It has no sidebar; only the logo and the account menu at the top right.

## When you see it

- When you sign in and you don't belong to exactly one active organization. If you belong to exactly one active organization, the app takes you straight into it.
- When you click **Back to welcome page** in the [organization switcher](./switch).
- When the organization you work in [is deactivated](./settings).
- When no organization is selected and you open **Home**.

## Steps

1. Read the **Hi** greeting with your name.
2. Under **Choose an organization to continue**, click the organization to enter. Under each name is your role in it.
3. Or click **Create a new organization** to [create one](./create).

If you don't belong to any organization yet, the page says: "No organization yet? Create one, or ask a manager to send you an invitation by email." When you have an invitation, you [accept it](./invitations) from the email.

## Suspended organizations

A deactivated organization appears dimmed, with a **Suspended** tag, and clicking it doesn't enter it. If all your organizations are suspended, the page says: "{name} is suspended. Contact the FeedbackHub system administrator to reopen it."

If an organization was deactivated while you were working in it, the page says: "{name} has just been suspended. What you just did wasn't carried out. Contact the FeedbackHub system administrator to reopen it."

## System administrators

If you are a FeedbackHub system administrator, the page has an extra **Organizations** link.

## Result

When you choose an organization, the page reloads on that organization's **Home**.

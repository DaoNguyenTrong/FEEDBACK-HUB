# Invitations

Send an invitation by email so that someone joins your organization with a role you choose in advance. The person doesn't need an account yet.

## Before you start

- You have selected the right organization (see [switch organization](./switch)).

## Invite someone

1. Click **Members** in the sidebar, then the **Invitations** tab.
2. Click **Invite member** at the top right (**Invite** on a phone). The **Invite member** panel opens.
3. Enter the **Email**. It can be sent to people who do not have a Hub account yet.
4. Choose a **Role**. A role you don't have enough permissions to give is switched off, with an explanation.
5. Click **Send invitation**. Click **Cancel** to discard.

Invitations expire after 7 days and can be revoked before they are used.

## See and manage the invitations you sent

The **Invitations** tab lists them, with a count. Each row shows the email, the role and the deadline: "Valid until …" or "Expired at …". An expired invitation also has an **Expired** tag.

- **Resend**: sends the invitation again to the same email. Available on valid and on expired invitations.
- **Revoke**: for a valid invitation. The app asks "Revoke the invitation to …?" and says the link in the email will no longer work; click **Revoke** to confirm.
- **Delete**: for an expired invitation; it is deleted at once, without asking.

When there are none, the page says "No pending invitations". Invitations you send appear here until they are used, revoked or expired.

## Accept an invitation

The invited person opens the link in the email. The **Invitation to** organization-name page shows the role they are invited as and the invitation's deadline.

- If they have no account: enter a **Name**, **Username** and **Password** (the email comes from the invitation and can't be changed), then click **Create account and join**. If they already have an account, they click **Already have an account? Sign in**.
- If they are signed in: click **Accept invitation**.

## Result

- Sending: the app says "Invitation sent to …" and the invitation appears on the **Invitations** tab.
- Resending: the app says "Invitation resent. The invitation is valid until …".
- When the person accepts, they join the organization with the chosen role and appear in the [members](./members) list.

## Common problems

- **"This person is already a member of …"**: this email already belongs to the organization; no need to invite again.
- **"Enter an email like name@company.com."**: correct the email.
- **"The invitation wasn't sent. Please try again."**: the email didn't go out. Click **Send invitation** again.
- **An invitation row has no buttons, only a sentence**: you may not change that invitation, for example "Only an Owner can revoke this invitation" or "You don't have every permission of the … role".
- **The invited person sees "This invitation has expired"**: ask an organization manager to send it again.
- **The invited person sees "This invitation can no longer be used"**: it may have been revoked, or they may have received a newer one. They open the latest invitation email.
- **The invited person sees "This invitation isn't for the account you are using"**: they click **Sign out to use another account**, then open the invitation again.
- **The invited person sees "Verify your email before using the invitation"**: they open the verification email in their inbox, then open the invitation again.
- **The invited person sees "… is suspended"**: the invitation is still valid; they can accept it once the organization is active again.

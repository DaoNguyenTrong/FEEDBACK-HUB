# Members

The **Members** page lists the people in the organization you work in. From here you change a member's roles, remove them from the organization, or leave it yourself.

## Before you start

- You have selected the right organization (see [switch organization](./switch)).
- Everyone in the organization can see the list. Changing roles and removing members needs the **Manage members** permission; which buttons show also depends on your permissions compared with the person on the row.

## See the list

Click **Members** in the sidebar. Each row shows a member's name, email and roles; your own row also says "(you)". If you don't have the **Manage members** permission, you only see your own email. Above the list are tabs: **Members** (with the head count), **Invitations** (if you have the **Manage members** permission) and **Roles** (if you have the **Manage roles** permission). The **Invite member** button at the top right (**Invite** on a phone) also shows only with the **Manage members** permission; see [Invitations](./invitations).

When the list is long, the bottom says "Showing … of … members". Click **Show more** to load more.

## Change a member's roles

1. On the member's row, click the three-dot button (**Actions for** their name), then choose **Change roles**. On your own row, click the **Change roles** button directly.
2. Tick the roles they should have. Every member needs at least one role.
3. Click **Save**. Click **Cancel** to discard.

A role you don't have enough permissions to give is switched off, with an explanation: "Only an Owner can select this role." or "You don't have every permission of this role."

## Remove a member from the organization

1. On the member's row, click the three-dot button, then choose **Remove from organization**.
2. The app asks "Remove … from …?" and says the person will no longer see the organization's data. Click **Remove from organization** to confirm.

You can't remove yourself; use **Leave organization**.

## Leave the organization

1. On your row, click **Leave organization**.
2. The app asks "Leave …?" and says you will no longer see the organization's data; to come back you will need a new invitation. Click **Confirm**.

## Result

- Changing roles: the app says "Changed …'s roles to …".
- Removing a member: the app says "Removed … from …" and the row disappears from the list.
- Leaving: you leave the organization and the app takes you to the welcome page.

## Common problems

- **A row has no buttons, only a sentence**: you may not change that row. The sentence gives the reason, for example "Only an Owner can change this row", "You don't have every permission of the … role" or "You don't have enough permissions to change this row".
- **"The organization needs at least one owner."**: you can't take away the last Owner. Give the **Owner** role to someone else first.
- **"You are the only owner. Give the owner role to someone else before you leave."**: do as it says, then leave.
- **"This item no longer exists. The list has been reloaded."**: someone else just changed or removed this person. Look at the list that has just been reloaded.
